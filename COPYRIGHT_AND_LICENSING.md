# Copyright and Licensing Checklist

### Application: Bhagavad gita as it is
**Author Attribution**: His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Srila Prabhupada  
**Publishing Body**: The Bhaktivedanta Book Trust (BBT)

---

## 1. Content Rights & Permissions Checklist

- [x] **Devotional Integrity**: No synthetic translations, modern interpretations, or altered texts are mixed into the scripture repository.
- [x] **Attribution**: Every verse, chapter, and purport explicitly attributes authorship to His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada.
- [x] **Development Seed Data**: Development seed data is clearly demarcated in `assets/data/gita_seed.json` with an explicit notice in the About screen.
- [x] **Artwork Attribution**: The official application artwork expects the authentic book-cover image (`assets/images/bhagavad_gita_cover.png`) and application icon (`assets/images/app_icon_source.png`).
- [ ] **Production BBT Licensing**: Before commercial distribution or public release of the complete 700-verse copyrighted translation and purports on Google Play or the Apple App Store:
  - Obtain written authorization or license agreement from the **Bhaktivedanta Book Trust (BBT)**.
  - Verify chapter titles and purports against the licensed 1972 or revised edition specified in the agreement.
  - Include the official BBT copyright notice and trademark acknowledgments in the About screen.

---

## 2. Scripture Sharing Guidelines

When users share a verse using the in-app Share button:
- The shared text contains:
  1. The application header (`Bhagavad gita as it is`).
  2. Canonical reference (`BG X.Y`).
  3. Sanskrit in Devanagari.
  4. Roman transliteration with diacritical marks.
  5. Translation.
  6. Direct attribution (`— His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Srila Prabhupada`).
- Sharing complies with fair-use and devotional study guidelines.
